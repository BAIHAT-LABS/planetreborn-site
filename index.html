Defend Earth, Restore Nature

Planet Reborn: Eco TD is an epic environmental tower defense game. Place environmental devices, deploy heroes, and clear pollution across 15 scenic campaign maps.

Support Privacy Policy

Gameplay

Play campaign maps on multiple difficulty modes. Progress, settings, and inventory are stored on your device. Signing in with Game Center or Play Games is optional.

In-App Purchases

Gem packs and paid heroes are sold as In-App Purchases through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Payment is processed by Apple or Google. We do not collect your full payment card details.
