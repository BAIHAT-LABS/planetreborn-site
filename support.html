Common topics

In-App Purchases

Gem packs and paid heroes in Planet Reborn: Eco TD are purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play. If an unlock does not appear after payment, force-quit and reopen the App, then contact us with the approximate purchase time and product name.

Refunds

Apple processes App Store payments. Request refunds through Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com). For Google Play, use your Play order history. You may copy us on the request so we can check whether the unlock was granted in Planet Reborn: Eco TD.

Game Center / Play Games

Sign-in in Planet Reborn: Eco TD is optional. Achievements may appear in the App first and sync to the platform when the corresponding IDs are configured in App Store Connect or Google Play Console.

Saved progress

Progress in Planet Reborn: Eco TD is stored on your device. Uninstalling the App or clearing app data may remove local saves. Contact us if you used Game Center or Play Games and need help understanding what can be restored.
