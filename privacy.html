Last updated: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Planet Reborn: Eco TD — Defend Earth, Restore Nature (“Planet Reborn: Eco TD” or “the App”) handles information. By using Planet Reborn: Eco TD, you agree to this Policy.

Planet Reborn: Eco TD — Defend Earth, Restore Nature is a mobile tower defense game published by BHL Games. For privacy requests about Planet Reborn: Eco TD, contact Ecodefense@baihatlabs.com.

  • Provide and maintain Planet Reborn: Eco TD gameplay and saved progress;

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your information to build advertising profiles for third-party ads.
